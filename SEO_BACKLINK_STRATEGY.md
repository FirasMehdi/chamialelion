# Le Lion backlink strategy

## Objective

Earn relevant, editorial links that introduce Le Lion to Tunisian consumers and food-sector buyers. Prioritize real relationships and referral traffic over raw link counts. Do not buy links, use automated submissions, or promise reciprocal links.

## Priority sources

1. **Business identity and local discovery**
   - Claim and complete the verified Google Business Profile and Bing Places listing.
   - Keep the business name, Boumhel El Bassatine address, telephone numbers, and website consistent across profiles.
   - Ask existing suppliers, distributors, and retail partners to include a link on their genuine partner or stockist pages.
2. **Tunisian food and trade organizations**
   - Identify relevant food-manufacturing, confectionery, export, and chamber-of-commerce directories in Tunisia.
   - Apply only where the company is eligible, and provide accurate company details and a useful website link.
   - Seek exhibitor or member profile links from trade fairs and associations the company actually joins.
3. **Retail, hospitality, and distribution partners**
   - Offer retailers, hotels, caterers, and distributors concise product information and approved images for their supplier pages.
   - Request an editorial link when a partner publishes a real product feature, stockist listing, or case study.
4. **Editorial and seasonal coverage**
   - Pitch Tunisian food, culture, business, and export publications with an original story about chamia and the brand’s documented history.
   - Prepare a press kit with approved product photographs, company contact details, and fact-checked background information.
   - Plan seasonal outreach around Ramadan and other relevant retail periods without sending mass, templated requests.

## 90-day execution

- **Weeks 1–2:** Confirm the canonical HTTPS website is live; prepare a fact sheet, press images, and a consistent company profile. Audit existing partner and directory listings.
- **Weeks 3–6:** Complete eligible business and trade profiles; contact existing distributors and retail partners individually about accurate supplier listings.
- **Weeks 7–10:** Pitch a small, researched list of relevant Tunisian food and business journalists with a specific, verifiable story angle.
- **Weeks 11–13:** Follow up once with interested contacts, publish partner features when earned, and review referral traffic and business enquiries.

## Measurement and safeguards

Track new referring domains, relevance and editorial context, referral visits, branded searches, and qualified wholesale enquiries. Review new links monthly and keep a record of the referring page and relationship. Do not claim certifications, ingredients, export markets, awards, or partnerships unless the company has verified them. Never pay for followed links or use private blog networks, link exchanges at scale, or automated directory blasts.
